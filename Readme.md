\# SOC Analyst Internship – Advanced Security Operations Task



\## Overview

This repository documents the practical and theoretical work completed as part of my \*\*SOC Analyst Internship training\*\*.  

The objective of this task was to develop hands-on experience in \*\*Security Operations Center (SOC) workflows\*\*, including log analysis, threat intelligence integration, incident escalation, alert triage, evidence preservation, and full attack-response simulation.



The project simulates real SOC operations where alerts are detected, analyzed, investigated, escalated, and documented.

\# 3. Incident Escalation Workflow



SOC teams follow a structured escalation process.



\### SOC Tier Structure



Tier | Responsibility

----|---------------

Tier 1 | Alert triage and initial investigation

Tier 2 | Deep investigation and containment

Tier 3 | Advanced threat analysis and malware research



\### Escalation Process



1\. Alert detected

2\. Initial triage

3\. Investigation

4\. Escalation to Tier-2

5\. Incident response actions

6\. Reporting and documentation
